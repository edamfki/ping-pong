The default schedule is the PDF of 6 Nov. It stays until you change an availability field, then the schedule is recomputed.
Type the names as in the player lists (first name, family name or full name), separated by commas. Pool matches of these players are always moved to days when they can play (a player who cannot play on two of the three pool days cannot be scheduled: he would have 4 matches in one day). A player never plays more than 2 matches a day; the round table is kept as far as possible (both matches of a round on the same day, rounds in order, level 1 vs level 2 last). Thursday and Friday matches can only be checked, because they depend on the results.
